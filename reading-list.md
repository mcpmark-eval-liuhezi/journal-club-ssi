# Journal Club Reading List — Surgical Site Infection & Postoperative Wound Outcomes

Theme: surgical site infection and postoperative wound outcomes. The anchor paper is entry #1, followed by five recent papers selected from PubMed sweeps on SSI epidemiology/outcomes and prevention (care bundles, antisepsis, timing of antibiotic prophylaxis).

## Papers

1. **Surgical Treatment Outcomes for Primary Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Single-Centre Retrospective Study of 2222 Patients (2006-2020)** *(anchor paper)*
   - PMID: 42633928
   - Journal: International wound journal
   - First author: Tokocin
   - Publication date: 2026

2. **Negative pressure wound therapy for the prevention of surgical site complications in emergency laparotomies: an updated systematic review and meta-analysis**
   - PMID: 42902330
   - Journal: World journal of emergency surgery : WJES
   - First author: Rey Chaves
   - Publication date: 2026

3. **Surgical site infections after cesarean delivery**
   - PMID: 41485839
   - Journal: American journal of obstetrics and gynecology
   - First author: Hayasaka
   - Publication date: 2026

4. **Are prophylactic antibiotics routinely necessary in laparoscopic adnexal surgery? A multicenter cohort study**
   - PMID: 42897337
   - Journal: Surgical endoscopy
   - First author: Miyata
   - Publication date: 2026

5. **Extending prophylactic antimicrobial use beyond 48 h postoperatively fails to reduce surgical site infections and other healthcare-associated infections in patients undergoing colorectal resection: a Propensity Score Matching study**
   - PMID: 42894129
   - Journal: BMC infectious diseases
   - First author: Yang
   - Publication date: 2026

6. **Risk factors for surgical site infection requiring reoperation after osteotomy around the knee: a multicenter retrospective study**
   - PMID: 42897581
   - Journal: BMC musculoskeletal disorders
   - First author: Arai
   - Publication date: 2026
